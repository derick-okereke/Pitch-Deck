## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined, quiet, and editorial visual language tailored for an institutional-grade fundraising platform and AI pitch simulator. Rejecting typical flashy AI marketing tropes—such as neon volumetric gradients, 3D clay illustrations, and hyperactive micro-interactions—the brand adopts an intentional, museum-grade aesthetic inspired by premium modern portfolio monographs.

The target audience spans two discerning personas: visionary startup founders seeking disciplined pitch perfection, and discerning angel/institutional investors demanding high-signal, substantiated discovery. The visual tone is composed, disciplined, and authoritatively calm.

Key stylistic pillars:
- **Quiet Editorial Architecture:** A sequence of large, soft-radius curved panels stacked over a tranquil neutral backdrop (`#EDEDED`), creating framed focal planes with generous whitespace.
- **Product-Led Typographic Dominance:** Crisp modern sans-serif typography set in high contrast against quiet monochrome surfaces, punctuated by miniature metadata indicators.
- **Selective High-Fidelity Signal:** A monochromatic base layered with purposeful electric blue accents for interactive focus and an unmistakable amber-orange token for verified milestone scores and readiness states.

## Colors

The palette relies on deliberate contrast hierarchies. The canvas sits on a calm, warm-neutral tone (`#EDEDED`), providing an architectural perimeter around pure white (`#FFFFFF`) and deep charcoal (`#111111`) panels.

- **Primary (`#1D4ED8`):** Clean, authoritative cobalt blue used strictly for foundational interactive targets, active tabs, progress trajectories, and live AI audio waveforms.
- **Secondary (`#111111`):** Deep carbon charcoal, replacing raw digital `#000000` to deliver softer high-contrast typography, primary founder action buttons, and dark product preview enclosures.
- **Tertiary (`#EF8328`):** Earned amber-orange. Reserved solely as an evaluation signal (Pitch-Readiness score rings, badge highlights, critical coaching callouts). Never used for decorative filler or generic text.
- **Neutral (`#EDEDED`):** The structural canvas background providing the editorial grounding for floating stacked containers.

Subtle borders (`#E2E2E2` and `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`) separate cards without relying on heavy ambient drop-shadows.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies on geometric discipline using `Instrument Sans`. The weight progression is intentionally restrained—relying heavily on Medium (500) for UI elements, SemiBold (600) for structural section titles, and Bold (700) for core display titles.

- **Headings:** Headlines use tighter negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and constrained line-heights to produce an authoritative, compact editorial block. Hero headlines must always break purposefully across 2 or 3 lines rather than spreading loosely across screen widths.
- **Body:** Body text is calibrated with comfortable line height (`1.55x` to `1.6x`) and dark grey color (`#6B7280`) to preserve an effortless reading cadence without harsh contrast vibration.
- **Labels & Metrics:** Utility labels, investor attributes, and telemetry points (e.g. `142 WPM`, `Verified Pitch-Ready`) leverage `label-sm` with slight positive tracking to ensure supreme legibility at small sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a centered page shell flanked by generous margins, echoing an art-directed print portfolio.

- **Grid & Panels:** The master canvas conforms to a maximum width of `1240px`, centered within the viewport over the `#EDEDED` base canvas with an outer desktop gutter margin of `2.5rem` (`40px`). Inside this shell, content organizes across a responsive 12-column grid.
- **Vertical Stack Rhythm:** Major sections exist as discrete, large rounded containers separated vertically by uniform `1.5rem` (`24px`) intervals, establishing a calm, module-by-module narrative progression.
- **Mobile Adaptations:** At breakpoints below `768px`, outer canvas margins contract to `1rem` (`16px`). Multi-column grids (such as the 3-step value columns and dual CTA pairs) stack vertically into full-width units with `space-md` gaps.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is achieved through tonal stacking and razor-sharp border definitions rather than deep or dramatic drop shadows.

- **Base Tonal Planes:** The foundational layer is the `#EDEDED` background. Elevated containers (`#FFFFFF` or `#111111`) sit directly on this canvas, bordered with a delicate 1px boundary (`#E2E2E2` on light, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` on dark).
- **Subtle Ambient Lift:** For floating detail elements—such as the Pitch-Readiness score card or active utility badges—an ultra-diffuse, tinted elevation shadow is applied:
  `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.03);`
- **Dark Surface Modules:** Dark inner preview components (such as the AI Pitch Room) create high contrast against white card surfaces, grounding the UI with a tactile, screen-within-screen feeling.

## Shapes

The design system establishes a consistent mathematical progression of corner radii:

- **Large Canvas Panels:** Outer section containers employ generous corner curvature between `32px` and `40px` (desktop) and `20px` to `24px` (mobile), creating a friendly, tablet-like enclosing silhouette.
- **Cards & Inner Modals:** Product preview cards, discovery tiles, and score modules use `16px` to `20px` radius.
- **Controls & Micro-Components:** Buttons, text input fields, and category tags utilize a controlled `10px` to `12px` radius. The system strictly avoids fully rounded circular pill buttons (`9999px`) in favor of structured, modern rounded rectangles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Founder Action:** Solid deep charcoal (`#111111`) fill with white text (`#FFFFFF`), `10px` border radius, padding `12px 24px`, font weight 600 (`label-md`). Hover transitions to `#262626`.
- **Secondary Investor Action:** Crisp white surface (`#FFFFFF`), 1px solid border (`#E2E2E2`), charcoal text (`#111111`), matching `10px` radius. Hover state introduces a quiet `#F4F4F5` fill.
- **Accent Brand Action:** Solid cobalt blue (`#1D4ED8`) reserved for contextual in-product interactions (e.g. "Join Pitch Session"). Hover transitions to `#1E40AF`.

### Header Module
Integrated directly within the top bounds of the first rounded container rather than a floating pinned bar. Contains a minimal typography-only wordmark (`Peekytoe`), centered text navigational links in `#6B7280` (hovering to `#111111`), and a restrained right-aligned action pair ("Sign in" and compact button).

### AI Pitch Room Preview Card
A dark preview block (`#111111`) featuring:
- Abstract avatar indicators for three simulated AI partners.
- An animated or static vector audio waveform rendered in cobalt blue (`#1D4ED8`).
- Monospace-styled live session timer (e.g., `02:18`) and real-time cadence indicators (`142 WPM`).
- 1px interior boundary (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).

### Pitch-Readiness Floating Module
A compact, high-focus card overlapping the dark preview surface:
- Prominent readiness metric (`78 / 100`) set in `score-metric`.
- Circular progress track featuring `#EF8328` as the active arc against a faint neutral path.
- Micro-coaching status chips indicating specific evaluation points ("Clear funding ask", "Strengthen market sizing") framed with 1px neutral borders.

### Discovery Result Cards
Curated investor search results styled as clean modular index cards:
- Top row: Company name (`headline-sm`) and concise one-line thesis (`body-sm`).
- Metadata row: Categorical pills (`Healthtech`, `Pre-seed`, `Lagos`) set in subtle neutral grey containers.
- Milestone badge: "Verified Pitch-Ready" tag with an `#EF8328` verification node.
- Action link: Compact text button ("View profile →").

### Proof & Ecosystem Strip
A tranquil horizontal layout showcasing 3 to 4 typographic proof points separated by quiet neutral dividers or subtle dot glyphs. Eliminates faux logos in favor of quiet, authentic capability statements.

## Owner-approved structural revision — 26 September 2026

This revision supersedes earlier guidance that used rounded outer section panels, a centered `1240px` page shell, and rounded action buttons.

- **Typography lock:** Instrument Sans is the owner-confirmed product typeface. Use its variable weights for display, body, labels, and controls. Do not replace or temporarily reinterpret this choice during later Impeccable passes unless the owner explicitly requests another typography review.
- **Full-bleed page architecture:** Primary page sections span the viewport width. Content retains generous internal gutters and may use a readable inner measure, but the page must not appear as a narrow stack of floating cards on a grey surround.
- **Rectilinear parent surfaces:** Headers, hero sections, major content sections, directories, detail panels, footers, cards, filter bars, and primary/secondary buttons use sharp corners. Separation comes from spacing, tonal changes, and fine horizontal rules.
- **Purposeful rounding only:** Preserve circles for progress rings, avatars, status nodes, timeline markers, and diagram geometry. Small verification/status tags may retain a slight radius so they remain visually distinct from actions.
- **Pathway infographic:** The landing-page pathway uses four cobalt circular stages with progressively rising fill, small black human figures, a black-node baseline, and centered step copy. This replaces the plain text timeline and is a durable signature element.
- **Professional restraint:** Do not compensate for removed corner radius with heavier shadows, glossy effects, or ornamental borders. Parent surfaces should feel architectural and precise. The approved page-wide blue atmosphere is the one exception to the earlier no-gradient rule.
- **Role-action connectors:** The landing hero identifies `For founders` and `For investors` above their respective actions. A single thin cobalt curve terminates in a downward-pointing arrow at each button. The motion draws once, then holds; reduced-motion shows the completed line. These are wayfinding marks, not decorative flourishes, and should not spread to routine action groups.
- **Homepage atmosphere:** The homepage uses a restrained, page-wide family of pale cobalt and sky-blue radial fields over a cool near-white base. Section surfaces remain translucent enough for the atmosphere to connect the page, while text and controls retain solid contrast. Avoid neon, glass blur, rainbow gradients, and isolated decorative orbs.
