## Direction contract

THESIS: The simulator is an inhabited network chamber, not a flat status diagram. It refuses generic avatar rows and makes the fictional panel feel spatial, attentive, and technologically credible.

OWN-WORLD: Deep carbon architecture, electric cobalt and cyan signal light, fine constellation lines, reflective black stage planes, and rectilinear interface chrome. Amber remains absent until earned feedback.

STORY: The founder enters a focused room, sees three distinct fictional investor positions, understands who is listening or speaking, records without distraction, and receives clear state feedback outside the visual effect.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A curved network ceiling and wall wrap a dark circular stage. Three recognizable luminous office chairs occupy left, centre, and right perspective positions at dominant scale. A restrained founder marker anchors the foreground; controls remain semantic beside the scene.

FORM: User-pinned reference extension in Operate mode, seed key user-pinned-reference. Tier 1 uses genuine WebGPU/TSL depth and bounded ambient motion; Tier 2 preserves the composition through lightweight DOM/SVG geometry and reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
